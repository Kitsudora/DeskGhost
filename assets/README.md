# Artwork and working process

For the artwork, all rights are reserved, but I still share the project files and the resulting work here.

I'm not a professional designer. A lot of my designs are simply whatever comes to mind. Here, I also want to show my working process.

## Files to look at

Early extensions made by the Agent are also kept here: [a card with the DONE sticker](SVG/card_example_done.svg), [the sticker-peeling animation](SVG/card_peel_demo.html), and [the top-bar and settings interaction demo](SVG/workspace_tabs_demo.html). Download the HTML demos with their neighboring assets and open them in a browser. Their interactions may differ from the current application.

The split assets used by the application are in [`desktop/web/assets/paper/`](../desktop/web/assets/paper/) and [`desktop/web/assets/stickers/`](../desktop/web/assets/stickers/).
